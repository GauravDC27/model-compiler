## poli-000
Question: cancelling an order

Which section answers it?
A) Refund window: Delivered items may be returned for a full refund within 30 days of delivery if the customer changed their mind. Grocery items cannot be returned for a change of mind.
B) Damaged or defective items: Items that arrive damaged, broken or defective are refunded within 90 days of delivery, including grocery items. A photo of the damage is requested.
C) Items not received: If an order has not been delivered 10 days after it was placed, the customer receives a full refund or a replacement. Before 10 days, ask them to wait.
D) Wrong item shipped: If we shipped the wrong item, colour or size, the customer gets a refund or exchange within 60 days of delivery, and we pay return shipping.
E) Expense approval: Expense reports under 500 dollars with a receipt are approved automatically. Reports without a receipt, over 500 dollars, or for personal items need manual review. For borderline reports the memo decides: finance pre-approval, client-billable costs, budgeted team plans, conference fees and IT-ticketed equipment are approved; a lost receipt, gift cards, personal purchases, reports split to stay under the limit and cash payments without an invoice need manual review.
F) Callbacks: Customers can ask for a phone callback. Callbacks are scheduled between 9am and 6pm on weekdays, in the customer's local time.
G) Address changes: Shipping addresses can be changed until the order ships. After shipping, the customer must contact the carrier.
H) Cancellations: Orders can be cancelled before they ship. Cancelling is permanent and refunds the full amount to the original payment method.
